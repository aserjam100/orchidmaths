# Numerator and denominator

The two numbers in a fraction have names. Each name tells you what the number counts.

## The denominator

In \(\frac{3}{8}\), the bottom number is 8. It is called the denominator.

The denominator tells you how many equal parts the whole is cut into. Here the whole is cut into 8 parts.

## The numerator

In \(\frac{3}{8}\), the top number is 3. It is called the numerator.

The numerator tells you how many of the parts you have. Here you have 3 of the 8 parts.

## Read a fraction

\(\frac{3}{8}\) is read "three eighths".

It means 3 out of 8 equal parts. The numerator is 3 and the denominator is 8.

A way to remember: the denominator is down at the bottom.
