# Subtracting fractions with the same denominator

When two fractions have the same denominator, take away the numerators and keep the denominator.

## Keep the denominator

To work out \(\frac{5}{8} - \frac{2}{8}\), look at the denominators. Both are 8.

You are taking eighths away from eighths, so the answer is in eighths.

The denominator of the answer is 8.

## Subtract the numerators

Subtract the numerators: 5 − 2 = 3.

So \(\frac{5}{8} - \frac{2}{8} = \frac{3}{8}\).

Five eighths take away two eighths leaves three eighths.

## Check by adding

Check the answer by adding back.

\(\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{8} = \frac{5}{8}\), which is the fraction you started with.

So \(\frac{3}{8}\) is right. Type it as 3/8.
