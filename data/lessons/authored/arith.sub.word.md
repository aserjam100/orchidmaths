# Subtraction word problems

Some stories take some away or ask how many are left. These are solved by subtracting.

## Find what you know

Read the story: You have 45 beads. You give away 18. How many are left?

The whole is 45. The part given away is 18. The question asks how many are left.

## Decide to subtract

Words like "give away", "left" and "how many more" often mean subtract.

A model helps. The whole is 45. One part is 18. The other part is not known.

```
[ 18      ][ ?           ]
<--------- 45 ---------->
```

Whole − part = other part, so subtract.

## Write the number sentence

Write the number sentence: 45 − 18 = 27.

Check: 27 + 18 = 45.

Answer the question in words: 27 beads are left.
