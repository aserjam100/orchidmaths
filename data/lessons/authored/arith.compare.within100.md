# Comparing numbers to 100

Place value tells you which number is greater. Compare the tens first.

## Compare the tens

Which is greater, 58 or 73?

Look at the tens digits first. 58 has 5 tens. 73 has 7 tens.

7 tens is more than 5 tens, so 73 is greater.

## When the tens are the same

Which is greater, 46 or 42?

Both have 4 tens, so look at the ones. 46 has 6 ones. 42 has 2 ones.

6 is more than 2, so 46 is greater.

A one-digit number like 9 has 0 tens, so it is smaller than any two-digit number.

## Order three numbers

Order 64, 28 and 61 from smallest to greatest.

Find the smallest: 28 has the fewest tens. Then 61 and 64 both have 6 tens. 61 has fewer ones, so it comes next.

The order is 28, 61, 64. Type it as 28,61,64.
