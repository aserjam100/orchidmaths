# Comparing numbers to 1000

To compare three-digit numbers, look at the hundreds first, then the tens, then the ones.

## Compare the hundreds

Which is greater, 418 or 392?

Look at the hundreds first. 418 has 4 hundreds. 392 has 3 hundreds.

4 hundreds is more than 3 hundreds, so 418 is greater, even though 392 has more tens.

## When the hundreds are the same

Which is greater, 572 or 547?

Both have 5 hundreds, so look at the tens. 572 has 7 tens. 547 has 4 tens.

So 572 is greater. If the tens are also the same, compare the ones.

## Order three numbers

Order 635, 653 and 629 from smallest to greatest.

All three have 6 hundreds. Compare the tens: 629 has 2 tens, 635 has 3 tens and 653 has 5 tens.

The order is 629, 635, 653. Type it as 629,635,653.
