# Division word problems

When a story shares a total into equal groups, divide. Some stories tell you the number of groups. Others tell you the size of each group.

## Sharing equally

Ahmad shares 20 sweets equally among 4 friends. How many sweets does each friend get?

The total is 20. The number of groups is 4. The question asks how many are in each group.

20 ÷ 4 = 5. Each friend gets 5 sweets.

## Putting into groups

Siti puts 20 sweets into bags of 4. How many bags are filled?

The total is 20. Each group has 4. The question asks how many groups there are.

20 ÷ 4 = 5. 5 bags are filled.

## Check by multiplying

Check the answer by multiplying back.

5 bags with 4 sweets in each bag: 5 × 4 = 20.

That is the total in the story, so the answer is right.
