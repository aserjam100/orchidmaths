## Coverage levels

- **full**: An Orchid concept practises the outcome as the syllabus states it.
- **partial**: An Orchid concept practises part of the outcome, or practises it without the syllabus's restrictions (number range, kind of fraction, and so on). The note says which.
- **none**: Nothing in Orchid practises this outcome yet.
