# Taking away numbers up to 9

Subtracting takes some away from a group. The answer tells you how many are left.

## Take away

Ravi has 9 marbles. Ravi gives away 3.

Show 9 counters. Take away 3 of them. Count what is left: 6.

So 9 − 3 = 6.

## Count back

To work out 8 − 3, start at 8 and count back 3 steps: 7, 6, 5.

The last number you say is the answer. So 8 − 3 = 5.

## Use an addition fact

Adding and subtracting are linked. To work out 7 − 4, ask: 4 plus what makes 7?

4 + 3 = 7, so 7 − 4 = 3.

You can check a subtraction this way too: 3 + 4 = 7.
