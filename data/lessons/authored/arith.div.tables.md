# Dividing using times tables

Every division fact has a multiplication fact that matches it. Use the times tables to divide.

## Two ways to divide

24 ÷ 4 can mean two things.

Sharing: share 24 equally into 4 groups. How many are in each group?

Grouping: put 24 into groups of 4. How many groups are there?

Both give the same answer, 6.

## Think of multiplication

To work out 35 ÷ 5, ask: 5 times what number makes 35?

Count in fives: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35. That is 7 steps.

So 5 × 7 = 35, and 35 ÷ 5 = 7.

## Check by multiplying

Check 30 ÷ 3 = 10 by multiplying back: 3 × 10 = 30.

You get the number you started with, so the answer is right.

The four facts 3 × 10 = 30, 10 × 3 = 30, 30 ÷ 3 = 10 and 30 ÷ 10 = 3 belong together.
