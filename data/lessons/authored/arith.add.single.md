# Adding numbers up to 9

Adding puts two groups together. The answer tells you how many there are altogether.

## Count on from the bigger number

To work out 3 + 6, start at the bigger number, 6. Then count on 3 more: 7, 8, 9.

The last number you say is the answer. So 3 + 6 = 9.

Starting at the bigger number means you count fewer steps.

## Make ten

To work out 8 + 5, think: 8 needs 2 more to make 10.

Break 5 into 2 and 3. Then 8 + 2 = 10, and 10 + 3 = 13.

So 8 + 5 = 13. Making ten works well when one number is 8 or 9.

## Use doubles

Doubles are easy to remember: 5 + 5 = 10, 6 + 6 = 12, 7 + 7 = 14.

To work out 6 + 7, use the double 6 + 6 = 12. 7 is one more than 6, so the answer is one more than 12.

So 6 + 7 = 13.
