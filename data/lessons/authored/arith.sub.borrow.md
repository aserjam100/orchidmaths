# Subtracting with regrouping

When there are not enough ones to take away, change 1 ten into 10 ones. This is called regrouping.

## Write the numbers in columns

To work out 52 − 17, write the bigger number on top. Tens go under tens and ones go under ones.

```
  5 2
− 1 7
-----
```

Start from the ones column on the right.

## Regroup 1 ten as 10 ones

In the ones column, 2 − 7 cannot be done. There are not enough ones.

Take 1 ten from the 5 tens. Now there are 4 tens. Add the 10 ones to the 2 ones to make 12 ones.

12 − 7 = 5. Write 5 in the ones column.

## Subtract the tens

Now subtract the tens. There are 4 tens left on top: 4 − 1 = 3.

```
  4 12
  5  2
−  1 7
------
  3  5
```

So 52 − 17 = 35. Check: 35 + 17 = 52.
