# Adding numbers within 1000

Three-digit numbers are added the same way as two-digit numbers: one column at a time, starting with the ones.

## Line up the columns

To work out 365 + 248, write hundreds under hundreds, tens under tens and ones under ones.

```
  3 6 5
+ 2 4 8
-------
```

Start adding at the ones column on the right.

## Regroup when a column makes 10 or more

Ones: 5 + 8 = 13. Write 3 and carry 1 ten.

Tens: 1 + 6 + 4 = 11. Write 1 and carry 1 hundred.

```
  1 1
  3 6 5
+ 2 4 8
-------
    1 3
```

## Add the hundreds

Hundreds: 1 + 3 + 2 = 6.

```
  1 1
  3 6 5
+ 2 4 8
-------
  6 1 3
```

So 365 + 248 = 613. Check that the answer makes sense: 365 is about 400 and 248 is about 200, and 613 is close to 600.
