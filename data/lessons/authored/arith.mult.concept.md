# Multiplying as equal groups

Multiplying is a quick way to add equal groups.

## Equal groups

Siti has 4 plates. There are 5 curry puffs on each plate.

Each plate has the same number. These are equal groups.

There are 4 groups, and there are 5 in each group.

## Add the groups

4 groups of 5 means 5 + 5 + 5 + 5.

Count in fives: 5, 10, 15, 20.

So 4 groups of 5 is 20.

## The multiplication sign

The sign × means "groups of".

4 groups of 5 is written 4 × 5. Read it as "four times five".

4 × 5 = 20.
