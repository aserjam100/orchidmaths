# What is a fraction

A fraction tells you how many equal parts of a whole you have.

## Equal parts

A whole can be cut into equal parts. Equal parts are all the same size.

A cake cut into 4 equal pieces has 4 parts. Each part is one quarter of the cake.

If the pieces are not the same size, they are not equal parts, and you cannot name them as a fraction.

## Count the parts

In the questions, a bar is shown like this. X is a filled part and _ is an empty part:

X X _ _ _

There are 5 parts in all. 2 of them are filled.

## Write the fraction

Write the filled parts on top and all the parts below:

\(\frac{2}{5}\)

Read it as "two fifths". In the answer box, type it as 2/5.
