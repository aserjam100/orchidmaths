# Times tables of 3 and 4

The tables of 3 and 4 come from counting in threes and fours. A fact you already know can help with a new one.

## Count in threes

The 3 times table counts in threes: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 30.

To work out 3 × 5, count 5 steps of three: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15.

So 3 × 5 = 15.

## Count in fours by doubling

The 4 times table counts in fours: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40.

Four is double two. So a 4 times fact is double the 2 times fact.

4 × 6: first 2 × 6 = 12, then double 12 to get 24.

## Use a fact you know

To work out 3 × 7, start from a fact you know: 3 × 5 = 15.

3 × 7 is two more threes: 15 + 3 + 3 = 21.

So 3 × 7 = 21. This works for any fact you cannot remember yet.
