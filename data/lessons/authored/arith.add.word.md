# Addition word problems

A word problem tells a short story. Find the numbers, decide what to do, and write a number sentence.

## Find what you know

Read the story: You have 24 stickers. Your friend gives you 13 more. How many do you have now?

The numbers you know are 24 and 13. The question asks how many there are now.

## Decide to add

Words like "more", "altogether" and "in all" often mean add.

A model helps. The two parts are 24 and 13. The whole is not known yet.

```
[ 24          ][ 13      ]
<------- whole ? ------->
```

Part + part = whole, so add.

## Write the number sentence

Write the number sentence: 24 + 13 = 37.

Then answer the question in words: You have 37 stickers now.

Type only the number, 37, in the answer box.
