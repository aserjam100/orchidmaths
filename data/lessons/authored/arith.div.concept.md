# Sharing equally

Dividing shares a number of things into equal groups.

## Share one at a time

Wei Jie has 12 sweets to share equally among 3 friends.

Give one sweet to each friend in turn, until no sweets are left.

Each friend gets 4 sweets.

## The division sign

The sign ÷ means "shared equally into".

12 shared equally into 3 groups is written 12 ÷ 3. Read it as "twelve divided by three".

12 ÷ 3 = 4.

## Check by multiplying

If each of 3 friends has 4 sweets, then 3 groups of 4 make 3 × 4 = 12.

That is the number of sweets you started with, so 12 ÷ 3 = 4 is right.
