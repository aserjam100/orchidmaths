# Adding fractions with the same denominator

When two fractions have the same denominator, the parts are the same size, so you can add them directly.

## Keep the denominator

To work out \(\frac{2}{7} + \frac{3}{7}\), look at the denominators. Both are 7.

The parts are sevenths. Adding sevenths to sevenths gives sevenths.

So the denominator of the answer is 7.

## Add the numerators

Add the numerators: 2 + 3 = 5.

So \(\frac{2}{7} + \frac{3}{7} = \frac{5}{7}\).

Two sevenths and three sevenths make five sevenths.

## Making one whole

If the numerator and the denominator are the same, the fraction is one whole.

\(\frac{3}{6} + \frac{3}{6} = \frac{6}{6}\), and \(\frac{6}{6} = 1\).

Type your answer as a fraction, like 5/7. An answer such as 6/6 or 1 is accepted for one whole.
