# Comparing fractions

Two kinds of fractions can be compared without drawing: unit fractions, and fractions with the same denominator.

## Unit fractions

A unit fraction has 1 on top, like \(\frac{1}{3}\) or \(\frac{1}{5}\).

A cake cut into 3 parts gives bigger pieces than a cake cut into 5 parts.

So \(\frac{1}{3} > \frac{1}{5}\). For unit fractions, the bigger the denominator, the smaller the fraction.

## Like fractions

Like fractions have the same denominator, like \(\frac{3}{8}\) and \(\frac{5}{8}\).

The parts are the same size, so compare how many parts there are. 5 parts is more than 3 parts.

So \(\frac{5}{8} > \frac{3}{8}\).

## Use the signs

The sign > means "is greater than". The sign < means "is less than". The open side faces the bigger number.

\(\frac{1}{4} < \frac{1}{2}\) because a half is bigger than a quarter.

In the answer box, type only the sign: > or <.
