# Subtracting numbers within 100

To subtract two-digit numbers, take away the tens and then the ones.

## Take away the tens, then the ones

To work out 68 − 25:

Take away the tens: 68 − 20 = 48.

Take away the ones: 48 − 5 = 43.

So 68 − 25 = 43.

## When there are not enough ones

To work out 52 − 17:

Take away the tens: 52 − 10 = 42.

Now take away 7 ones. 42 has only 2 ones, so count back past a ten: 42 − 2 = 40, then 40 − 5 = 35.

So 52 − 17 = 35.

## Check with addition

Subtraction can be checked by adding.

If 52 − 17 = 35, then 35 + 17 must make 52.

35 + 17 = 52, so the answer is right.
